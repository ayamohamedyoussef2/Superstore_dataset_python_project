Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore dataset to identify key patterns in sales, profitability, products, customers, shipping, discounts, and geographical performance.
The project combines Python for data cleaning and analysis with Power BI for interactive business dashboards.

Tools Used

Python — Data cleaning, transformation, and analysis  
Pandas — Data manipulation and aggregation  
Power BI — Interactive dashboards and business intelligence

Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and transformed before analysis. The project includes the following derived fields:
Shipping Day Delay
Discount Level
Profitability Status
Profit Margin
Date fields were also converted into appropriate date formats, and the dataset was checked for data quality issues.

Business Questions
The analysis investigates:
1. How do sales and profit vary across product sub-categories? — Shows which sub-categories perform best.


2. How does profit vary across shipment modes? — Shows which shipping methods are more profitable.


3. Which top 5 products generate the most revenue? — Identifies the main products driving revenue.


4. Which months or quarters have the highest sales? — Helps identify seasonal sales trends.


5. Which regions or states make the least profit? — Identifies underperforming locations.


6. Which customer group spends the most per order? — Shows the customer segment with the highest order value.


7. Do bigger discounts bring in more sales volume? — Shows whether higher discounts increase sales volume.


8. Which categories and sub-categories generate the most sales and profit? — Identifies the strongest product groups.


9. How do sales and profit change over time? — Tracks business performance over time.


10. Which categories and sub-categories generate the most sales and profit? — Shows the product groups bringing in the most revenue and profit.


11. How does discount level relate to profit? — Shows how discounts are associated with profitability.


Power BI Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview using interactive filters to show:
Total Sales,
Order Count,
Profit Margin,
Total Quantity,
Sales and Profit by Subcategory,
Profit by Shipment Mode,
Top 5 Products by Revenue and
sales per order by customer segment

Key Outcome

The project demonstrates how raw transactional data can be transformed into meaningful business insights through data preparation, exploratory analysis, visualization, and interactive business intelligence reporting.
